# Tilford's Free Shelf

## The Inner Room — free app download

**The Secret Teachings of Jesus — The Inner Room**, by Robert Tilford.
Free. No account, no ads, no testers, no gate. Download the APK and install it.

## Downloads

- **the-inner-room-api36.apk** — current build (targets Android API 36). For recent phones and tablets. SHA-256 in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- **the-inner-room-api33.apk** — earlier build (API 33), signed with the INNERROO release key. Kept for older devices.
- The matching `.aab` bundles are shelved alongside for the record; stores that want the bundle can have it. Readers want the APK.

## How to install

1. Download the APK to your Android phone or tablet.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or files app for this one file.
3. Install, open, read. That is all.

## What it is

The secret teachings of Jesus from Matthew 6 — prayer, giving, and fasting in the inner room, in clear English, from Robert Tilford's Clear English Bible work. Bare text, plainly presented. Nothing sold that is sacred: the app is free, as the work has always been given.

## Why it lives here

Google Play requires twelve strangers to test an app for fourteen days before a new personal account may publish. This shelf requires nobody's permission. Archive.org is the vault; this shelf is the storefront.
